Installing it

Do this on the phone itself. It takes about two minutes.

  1. Tap the download button above. Your browser will warn you about the file type — that is normal for an app that does not come from the Play Store.
  2. When it finishes, tap Open.
  3. Android will say it is not allowed to install unknown apps. Tap Settings, then turn on Allow from this source, then press back.
  4. Tap Install, then Open.
  5. The app walks you through the rest: microphone permission, downloading the speech models over Wi-Fi, and the cover screen setting.

The first run downloads about 460 MB of speech-recognition models, plus a chat model if you choose one. Do it on Wi-Fi. After that the phone never needs the internet to record or transcribe.

What it does with what it hears

Recordings never leave the phone. Speech becomes text using the phone's own processor. There is no cloud transcription and nothing to sign in to.

Nothing is sent without you. If you later connect email or calendar, the app writes a draft and waits for you to tap approve. It cannot send on its own.

The bigger AI is off by default. If you switch it on and add your own API key, only text is ever sent — never audio.
